Player View - local tangent map

The player is always at the centre; only a local patch of radius R is drawn and the rest stays under geometric fog. The coordinate grid is warped by the local first fundamental form (E, F, G) -- a hint that the world is not flat.

Move: WASD or arrow keys (hold to walk). 1 ruler · 2 angle gauge · 3 curvature · 4 pointer · G god view · Esc close god view.
